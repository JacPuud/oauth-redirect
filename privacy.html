Social HQ - Privacy Policy

Social HQ is a private desktop application that runs locally on its owner's computer. It is used by its single owner to view statistics of, and to schedule posts to, the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram.

Data accessed: the account's public profile name and identifier; the list of the owner's own videos/posts with view, like, comment and share counts; and, where the owner grants it, permission to upload videos that the owner selects.

How data is used: only to display statistics and to publish content chosen by the owner. All data is stored only in a local database on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server operated by the developer, and it is not shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party.

Access tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time in the security settings of the respective platform. Removing an account in the application deletes its stored data.

Contact: through the GitHub profile that publishes this page.
